1. About Mahadum360

Mahadum360 is the first Nigerian language learning platform that brings online lessons and gamification together with in-person practice sessions with family at home and at school, connecting parents, teachers and family guides in one place to drive learning outcomes for children. "Mahadum" means university in Igbo, and that is what we bring you: a full learning space in your pocket. Learn to speak, read and understand Nigerian languages and English, Nigeria’s lingua franca, in short, easy lessons. Languages of Nigeria’s neighbouring countries and trade partners are planned for future releases. Learn today on our web app from any smartphone, tablet or computer. Our mobile app and SMS shortcode access are planned for future releases, so you will be able to learn from any phone, even without a smartphone or data. Learn at your own pace, anytime, anywhere. Mahadum360 has language learning all round.

Mahadum360 is a company registered in Nigeria with RC number 9601595, whose registered office is at 3rd Floor, 927/928 Bishop Aboyade Cole Street, Victoria Island, Lagos, Nigeria ("Mahadum360", "we", "us"). You can contact us at support@mahadum360.com.

3. Who can use the service

Every account must include an adult. Every Individual, Family and School account must be held or managed by at least one adult aged 18 or over. Only that adult can pay for services or enter a learner into competitions.

Learners under 18 use supervised profiles rather than independent logins. Only a parent or legal guardian can create the account, add the learner profile and generate the child's access code. Children cannot sign up themselves and cannot make payments. No one under 18 can be a stand-alone user. Every learner under 18 uses Mahadum360 only as a supervised learner profile inside a Family or School account, and cannot pay for any service or enter competitions; the adult account holder does this for them.

Learners aged 13 to 17 are minors and are not recognised as able to enter into legally binding contracts, so the rule above applies to them in full: they cannot open their own account and may join only as supervised learner profiles in a parent’s or legal guardian’s Family account or in a School account.

Age checks and control measures: at sign-up, every user must enter their date of birth. Anyone under 18 is stopped and directed to a parent or legal guardian, who must create a Family account, confirm parental consent and add the learner as a supervised learner profile. Learners under 18 enter their learning space only with an access code generated by the parent. Paid plans can be bought only by an adult account holder, and competition entries for learners under 18 must be approved from the linked adult account. We do not ask for identity documents at sign-up. We may ask any account holder for proof of age or identity when there is a reason to check, for example if someone tries to change a date of birth to make themselves 18 or over, if an account is reported or appears to belong to a child, or before a cash referral payout. We may suspend or close an account where age or identity information is false.

Learners aged 18 and over may create their own account and use all features.

Payments: only account holders aged 18 or older can buy a plan, top up a wallet or make any payment. Learners under 18 cannot make payments; paid plans for under-18s are bought by the linked parent or guardian.

Schools and organisation accounts may only be created by adults with authority to act for the organisation. Only schools duly registered with the appropriate authority may open a School account (see Section 10).

The parent, guardian or authorised organisation is responsible for creating and supervising each learner profile and for giving any required consent.

Parents remain responsible for supervision: we have built safeguards for learners under 18, including supervised profiles, parent-only payments, parent approval of rewards, private profiles and controls over who can review recordings and photos. No online service can remove every risk, so parents and guardians remain responsible for monitoring their child’s activity on Mahadum360, including time spent learning, the recordings and photos their child submits, and their child’s contact with family guides and teachers. Please report any concern to us promptly at support@mahadum360.com. Nothing in this clause limits our own responsibilities under applicable law.

4. Accounts and learner profiles

Mahadum360 offers the following account types. Every account type must include at least one adult aged 18 or over, who is responsible for the account, payments and competition entries.

Individual user: one adult aged 18 or over who learns with one personal learner profile and is responsible for the account and all payments.

Family user: a household account held by at least one parent or legal guardian aged 18 or over, with up to 6 learner profiles for members of the family. Every family member under 18 uses a supervised learner profile. The adult account holder manages consent, payments, rewards and competition entries for every profile in the family.

School user: a school duly registered with the appropriate authority and holding a valid certificate of registration, acting through authorised adult administrators and teachers. A School account is used for students on the school’s roster, under the school’s agreement with us.

Institutions, such as universities, cultural organisations and government programmes, use accounts set up under a separate written agreement.

Account responsibilities: account holders must give accurate information, including date of birth, keep passwords and learner access codes secure, and tell us promptly about any unauthorised access. Account holders are responsible for activity on their account and on the learner profiles they manage, unless the law says otherwise.

Learning levels: each course is organised into levels, starting with Level 0, which is free. Level names, content and the number of levels may change as the curriculum grows.

9. Coins, rewards, referrals and competitions

Coins, XP, badges, hearts, streak shields and similar learning rewards are platform features, not cash or stored value, unless a specific promotion clearly states otherwise. They cannot be exchanged for money and are lost if the account is closed. Chore coins are released only after parent approval.

Referral programme. Every account holder receives a unique referral code. You can share your code with people outside your own family or school group.

You earn only after a purchase. You do not earn anything for sharing your code, sending invitations, or for someone signing up or using the Free plan. You earn a referral reward only when a person signs up with your code and makes a qualifying paid purchase on Mahadum360.

How much you earn: you earn a percentage, shown on your Refer & earn page or stated in your contract or organisation agreement, of the referred person’s first qualifying subscription payment (monthly or annual). Renewals, later purchases, data top-ups and coins do not earn a further reward.

School referral rewards (first enrolment only): for schools, the qualifying payment is the school’s first paid enrolment invoice (registration and student fees for the first academic year, excluding VAT). Enrolment for later academic years is a renewal on the same school account (for example, “Re-enrolment 2027/28”) and does not earn a further reward. Closing and reopening a school, or re-registering it under a new name, does not restart the reward. Ongoing school engagement is handled by a separate school programme, not by referral rewards.

Schools and teachers do not earn referral rewards on students registered on their own roster, or on those students’ purchases.

Parents and guardians do not earn referral rewards on their own children or on members of their own family account. They do earn on people they refer from outside their family group.

No self-referrals: you cannot earn by referring yourself, a second account you control, or accounts created to collect rewards.

Holding period and payout: rewards are held for 14 days after the qualifying purchase before they clear. The minimum payout is ₦5,000. Cash payouts are made only to verified account holders aged 18 or older, to an account in their own name.

Reversals: a reward is cancelled or reduced if the qualifying payment is reversed, charged back, corrected as a billing error or found to be fraudulent.

Fair sharing: do not spam, make false or misleading claims about Mahadum360, or pretend to speak for us. We may suspend referral earnings, cancel uncleared rewards and close referral access for fraud or misuse.

Taxes: you are responsible for any tax that applies to your referral earnings.

Changes: we may change or end the referral programme. Rewards that have already cleared will still be paid.

Competitions will be announced on Mahadum360 from time to time. The terms and conditions for each competition, including who can enter, entry dates, judging and prizes, will be announced with that competition and apply alongside these terms.
