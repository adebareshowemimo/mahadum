Write a Professional Email

Hello, students! Put your email-writing skills into practice.

Use the step-by-step guide we reviewed in class to type your own email. Follow the proper format for professional communication.

Assessment requirements

Your email will be graded using our 50-point assessment scale. Include all five components below.

Your five-part checklist

  1. Subject Line
    Clearly identifies the purpose of your email.
  2. Professional Greeting
    Properly addresses the recipient.
  3. Body of the Email
    Clearly communicates your message using complete sentences and professional language.
  4. Closing Statement
    Ends your message politely and professionally.
  5. Signature
    Includes your full name.

Before you submit

Review the guide. Check that all five components are included, then read your email once more for clarity and professionalism.

Remember: Your goal is to communicate clearly, professionally, and effectively. Take your time, review your work, and submit your completed assessment when you are finished.

Good luck, students!
