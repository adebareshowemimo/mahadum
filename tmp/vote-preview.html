Who can vote?

Anyone with a Mahadum360 account and a verified email address. Browse the languages without signing in, then create an account or sign in to make your choice count. Each account gets one vote.

I chose a language when I signed up. Does it count?

Your choice is saved. It counts automatically once you verify your email. You do not need to vote again.

Can I change my vote?

Each account gets one language vote. Review your choice before confirming; a recorded vote cannot be changed here.

What happens when a language reaches the goal?

At 10,000 verified votes for a language, work starts, with a target of being ready in 60 days. Suggestions for other languages are reviewed by the team.
